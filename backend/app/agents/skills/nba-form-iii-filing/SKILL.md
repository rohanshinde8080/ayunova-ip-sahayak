# National Biodiversity Authority (NBA) Form III Filing Playbook

## 1. Statutory Mandate
Under **Section 6 and Section 19 of the Biological Diversity Act, 2002** (as amended by the Biological Diversity (Amendment) Act, 2023):
> *"No person shall apply for any intellectual property right, by whatever name called, in or outside India for any invention based on any research or information on a biological resource obtained from India without obtaining the previous approval of the National Biodiversity Authority before grant of such intellectual property right."*
Section 19 establishes the mandatory application process and submission of Form III to the National Biodiversity Authority (NBA).

Failing to secure NBA Form III approval prior to patent grant can result in:
- **Patent Revocation** under Section 64(1)(q) of The Patents Act, 1970.
- **Statutory Penalties** under Section 55 of the Biological Diversity Act.


---

## 2. Entity Determination: Section 3 vs. Section 7

| Entity Type | Section | Requirement |
|---|---|---|
| **Foreign National / Non-Resident Indian** | Section 3(2)(a)-(b) | Mandatory prior approval from NBA for accessing biological resources AND Form III before patent grant. |
| **Indian Company with Non-Indian Shareholding** | Section 3(2)(c) | Requires prior NBA approval for biological resource access AND Form III. |
| **Indian Citizen / Indian Entity** | Section 7 | Prior intimation to the concerned State Biodiversity Board (SBB). Form III is mandatory before obtaining patent grant if commercialized. |
| **Registered AYUSH Practitioners & Local Vaids** | Section 7 (Exemption) | Exempted from prior intimation to SBB for accessing biological resources for traditional healthcare purposes. |

## 3. Access & Benefit Sharing (ABS) Fee Computation
Under the NBA ABS Regulations:
- Benefit-sharing percentage typically ranges between **0.1% to 0.5%** of the annual gross ex-factory sale value of the product derived from the biological resource.
- In the case of direct patent licensing or assignment of IPR to a third party, the NBA may levy **3% to 5%** of the royalty or licensing fee received.

### Calculation Formula:
$$ABS\ Fee = Gross\ Ex\text{-}Factory\ Turnover \times ABS\ Rate\ (0.1\%\ to\ 0.5\%)$$

---

## 4. Step-by-Step Form III Application Workflow
1. **Filing Window**: Form III can be submitted immediately after filing the patent application (at provisional or complete specification stage). Must be granted **prior to final patent grant**.
2. **Online Portal**: Access the official NBA portal (`absefiling.nic.in`).
3. **Documents Required**:
   - Complete Patent Specification as filed with CGPDTM.
   - Official Patent Application Filing Receipt (with 12-digit CBR Number).
   - Source and geographical origin of biological resources (GPS coordinates, village, taluka, state).
   - Local Biodiversity Management Committee (BMC) consultation report (if available).
   - Proof of statutory filing fee payment (INR 10,000 for Indian individuals, higher for corporate entities).
4. **Draft Agreement Execution**: Upon approval, execute the formal Access and Benefit Sharing (ABS) Agreement with the NBA Secretary.
5. **Intimation to Patent Office**: Submit the signed NBA Approval Letter and ABS Agreement to the Patent Controller to clear the pre-grant requirement.
